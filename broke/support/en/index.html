FAQ

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?
In the app, go to Settings → Remove Ads → Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Can I play without an internet connection?
Yes. It is an offline, single-player game. You can play without a connection; ads only appear when you are online.

I deleted the app and lost my progress.
Your game is saved only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress too, and backup or moving to another phone is not supported.

How do I change the language?
The app follows your phone's language. It supports Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). You can also change the language for this app alone in your iPhone's Settings → Apps → Broke Escape → Language.

Why does money show in my currency?
Money is converted to the currency of your language at a fixed rate. The game itself is set in a Korea-like world, so things like salaries and home prices follow Korean standards.

How do I turn off vibration or the event art?
In the app's Settings, turn off Vibration or Big event & news art.

Where can I change ad privacy settings?
Where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), go to Settings → Ad privacy settings in the app. You can change tracking permission in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
