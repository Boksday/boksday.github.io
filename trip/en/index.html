Just what a trip needs

Convert, log and check, all in one app.

  • Side-by-side conversionPick a destination and its currency sits next to yours. The keypad does math too.
  • One-tap expense logSave an amount with a category such as Food or Transport, plus a note.
  • Cash left, tooSee cash and card separately, and how much exchanged cash is left.
  • Works offlineRates are saved on the device and built into the app, so it works from the first launch.
  • Home and Lock Screen widgetsA trip widget and a rates widget. Choose currencies and background colors.
  • 166 currencies · 5 languagesEnglish, Korean, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), with dark mode.

Your records stay on your phone

No account. Expenses are never sent to a server; they stay on your phone.

Move to a new phone with a single backup file, or export a CSV for spreadsheets.

Rates are mid-market reference rates. Bank and card rates and fees may differ.
