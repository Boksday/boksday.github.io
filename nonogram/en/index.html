One cell at a time

New to nonograms? Start with the tutorial.

  • A picture blooms into colorEach number is a run of filled cells. Fill them all and a pixel picture appears in color.
  • From 5×5 to 30×30Start with the 5×5 tutorial and work up through 10×10, 15×15, 20×20, 25×25 and the 30×30 Master boards.
  • Tap or drag to fillSwitch between Fill and Mark X. Drag your finger to fill a whole line at once.
  • 3 hearts, 3 hintsEach mistake costs a heart. Stuck? A hint shows one cell the current clues already settle.
  • Three medalsCleared, No mistakes and No hints. Collect them for every picture.
  • Big boards made easyPinch to zoom on 25×25 and 30×30 boards. Dark mode, five languages and vibration on or off.
