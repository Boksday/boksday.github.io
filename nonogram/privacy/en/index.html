BySide Nonogram Privacy Policy

Effective: October 13, 2026

BySide Nonogram (the "app", iOS and Android) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Information the app collects

The app has no account or sign-in, and it never sends your information to a server or database run by us. We receive no information from the app.

Your solved pictures, current game, medals and display, language and vibration settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not backed up or synced to other devices.

2. Downloading puzzles (GitHub Pages)

The app downloads the puzzle list and puzzle files from GitHub Pages (boksday.github.io), run by GitHub, Inc. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information such as the time of the request. GitHub processes it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement; we do not receive it.

3. Game Center (iOS)

Only if you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the app sends your medal count (for leaderboards) and achievements to Apple Game Center. Leaderboards show your Game Center nickname. Apple processes this information under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can turn off Game Center in the Settings app.

4. Purchases (Remove Ads)

The Remove Ads purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never receive card numbers or other payment details; the app only checks on your device whether the store reports a purchase. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal.

5. Information collected by third parties for ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner at the bottom of every screen, and, if you choose it when you run out of hearts, a rewarded ad that gives you one heart. AdMob may collect and process the following to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

6. Ad consent and opting out of personalized ads

  • Where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your ad privacy consent with Google's consent tool (UMP), and you can change it any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options (shown only where consent is required).
  • iOS: choose whether to allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you do not allow it, only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Retention and deletion

Records and settings stored on your device are deleted when you delete the app. We keep no information of our own. Information collected by third parties (Google, GitHub) is kept under their policies.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed primarily at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea) and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
