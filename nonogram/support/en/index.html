FAQ

How do I play?
The numbers beside each line are the lengths of runs of filled cells in that line. "3 1" means three filled cells in a row, at least one gap, then one more. Use Mark X on cells you know are empty, and drag your finger to fill a whole line at once.

What happens when I run out of hearts?
Each mistake costs a heart, and the game stops after three. Choose Watch an ad for 1 heart to keep going, or Start over.

How do hints work?
You get 1 free hint per picture, and can watch an ad for more. A hint shows one cell the current clues already settle. Solve without hints to earn the No hints medal.

The 25×25 and 30×30 boards are too small.
Spread two fingers to zoom in and drag with two fingers to move around. Pinch to zoom back out.

My progress is gone after deleting the app.
Progress is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it too, and backup or moving it to another phone is not supported.

How do I turn off vibration?
Go to Settings → Vibration in the app.

How do I change the language or appearance (light/dark)?
Choose Language and Appearance in the app's Settings. You can also leave them to follow your device.

Where are the ad privacy options?
Where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), use Settings → Ad privacy options in the app. On iPhone you can also use Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, Settings → Google → Ads.

A puzzle looks wrong (more than one answer, or a broken picture).
Email ekqlszzzz@naver.com with the board size and a screenshot. We will check and fix it.
