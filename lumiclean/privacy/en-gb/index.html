Lumiclean (the "App", for Android and iOS) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

At a glance

  • Your photo and video files are never sent to a server. Cleanup, compression and enhancement all happen on your phone.
  • There is no sign-up or login, and we run no server of our own.
  • Only on iPhone, when you view a photo full screen, the App sends the photo’s coordinates to Apple to look up the place name.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and you choose whether they are personalised.

1. Information the App collects

The App has no sign-up or login, and we run no server. Your photo and video files are not sent to us.

The App uses Google LLC’s Firebase Analytics to understand usage and improve the App. Automatically collected events such as app opens, sessions and automatic purchase events, sessions and automatic purchase events, an app instance identifier, device and operating system information, and approximate region inferred from IP address may be sent to Google; we can view analytics results. Photo and video files are not included in analytics. Analytics collection is off by default and is restricted according to regional consent status. See the Google Privacy Policy.

The App reads your photo library to find similar photos, screenshots and large files. Photo analysis, compression and enhancement all happen on your device. The AI model used for enhancement (Real-ESRGAN) is built into the App, so your photos never need to leave your phone.

On iPhone, if the original of a photo you enhance or compress is stored only in iCloud Photos, iOS may download it from your iCloud. That transfer is between you and Apple and does not pass through us.

Enhanced photos and compressed copies are saved to your photo library. When you delete photos, your phone asks you to confirm, and on iOS deleted photos move to Recently Deleted.

2. Photo location

Photos can contain the coordinates of where they were taken. On iPhone, when you view a photo full screen, the App shows that place as a neighbourhood name. To do this it sends only the photo’s coordinates to Apple’s place-name lookup service. The photo file and other information are not sent.

Place names the App receives are kept on your device, up to 500, so the same place is not looked up twice. Apple handles these requests under the Apple Privacy Policy.

On Android the App does not show place names and does not send coordinates off your device. On any device, the App never asks for or uses your current location.

3. Information collected by third parties for ads

The App is free and uses Google AdMob by Google LLC to show ads. AdMob may collect and process the following information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Collected by
Google LLC (AdMob)
Data
Advertising ID (Android advertising ID; iOS IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and OS information, app usage such as ad views and clicks, approximate location inferred from IP
Purpose
Showing ads and personalised ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The App also downloads a public promotion list and promotional images and videos for our apps from boksday.github.io (GitHub Pages), and caches them on your device. No photos from your library are sent for this purpose. The hosting provider may receive your IP address and network request metadata. Tapping a promotion opens an external store link.

4. Opting out of personalised ads

  • Android: go to Settings → Google → Ads on your phone to delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • iOS: you can choose whether to allow tracking on first launch, and change it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland can choose their ad privacy consent on first launch. Users in some US states can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. In both cases you can change your choice anytime in the App under Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • If consent cannot be confirmed or tracking is not allowed, the App requests only non-personalised ads.

5. Payments (Premium)

Premium purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. Premium offers monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a lifetime, non-consumable purchase. Neither the App nor we receive card numbers or other payment details. The App checks the Premium entitlement reported by the store and stores that status, plan and expiry date on your device so it can be used offline.

6. App lock

When App lock is on, the App asks for Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or your device passcode when you open it. Your phone’s operating system does the check, and the App receives only whether it succeeded. The App never reads or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode, and it has no passcode of its own.

7. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following only on your device. It contains no photo files, and we cannot see it.

Cleanup history
IDs of photos you set aside to delete or chose to keep, how many you deleted and how much space you freed, groups you chose to keep
Analysis results
File size of each photo, similar-photo results, records of compressed copies and space saved by compression
Place names
iOS only. Rounded coordinates and neighbourhood names, up to 500
Settings
App lock, appearance, language, which tips you have seen, Premium status, plan and expiry date

8. Permissions

Photo location

Used only when App lock is on.
Tracking

Used for ads, downloading our app promotions, purchase checks and iPhone place names.

The App does not ask for location or microphone permission. You can change or turn off photo access anytime in your phone’s settings.

9. Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device is removed when you delete the App. Photos saved to your photo library stay there after you delete the App, and you can remove them in your Photos app. We keep no information of our own. Information collected by third parties (Google, Apple, GitHub, the stores) is kept according to their policies.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed primarily at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea) and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Your rights

All information the App stores is on your device, so you can review and delete it yourself. For ad-related information, you can opt out of personalised ads as described in section 4.
